Taken AFTER the wallet run ended (10:03) with NO maestro/java process running, so this is

The close control is the clickable node bounds=[42,1403][105,1466], centre (73,1434). It has

  round 2: 1s_after_close=0   5s_after_close=1   <-- returned
  round 3: 1s_after_close=0   5s_after_close=0   <-- finally stayed closed

CONCLUSION SUPPORTED BY THIS EVIDENCE: the sheet is genuinely dismissed by the close X and the
app re-presents it several times before it stays closed. It is NOT a hierarchy-read race and
NOT a wrong coordinate.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE SUITE: subflows/clear-overlays.yaml bounds its NPS recovery to 3 attempts
(correctly - recovery must never be unbounded). The observed app behaviour needs roughly four
dismissals, so the bounded recovery is exhausted and the guard
  assertNotVisible "(?s).*would you recommend benefiting from Sakani.*"
fails. That guard was NOT relaxed and the retry count was NOT raised: doing either would hide
the behaviour instead of reporting it.
